# Traders World AI — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Goal

Build an AI Trading Copilot that becomes the trader's permanent companion.

The AI should not replace the trader.

Instead, it should increase execution quality, reduce emotional mistakes, teach the complete methodology, and continuously improve using accumulated knowledge.

---

# Problem Statement

Most traders fail because they:

- Enter too early
- Enter too late
- Break rules
- Trade emotionally
- Forget the strategy
- Cannot review thousands of historical examples

The strategy is often good.

Execution is poor.

Our AI exists to solve execution.

---

---

# User Journey

## Beginner

The user joins the platform.

The AI teaches:

1. Market structure
2. Trend
3. Liquidity
4. A+ Zones
5. Confirmation candles
6. Risk management
7. Psychology

After each lesson, the AI asks questions to verify understanding.

---

## Intermediate Trader

The trader opens the dashboard.

The AI checks:

- Higher timeframe bias
- A+ zones
- Session
- Confirmation
- Risk

Before every trade the AI asks:

- Why this trade?
- Which rule is satisfied?
- Where is invalidation?
- What is the target?
- Is this A+ or FOMO?

If a rule is missing, the AI advises waiting.

---

## Advanced Trader

The AI becomes a research assistant.

Questions it should answer include:

- Win rate of each setup
- Historical success by session
- Best RR
- Average holding time
- Backtest results
- Psychological mistakes
- Performance reports

# Non-Negotiable Rules

1. AI never predicts markets.
2. AI always explains every recommendation.
3. Every answer references documented strategy rules.
4. Knowledge is version controlled.
5. The system is modular and provider-independent.
6. Documentation is written before implementation.
