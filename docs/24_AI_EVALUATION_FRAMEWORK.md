# Traders World AI — AI Evaluation Framework

## Purpose

Define a repeatable process for measuring and improving the quality, reliability, and consistency of the AI Copilot over time.

---

# Objectives

- Measure AI quality objectively
- Detect regressions
- Improve reasoning
- Validate knowledge usage
- Build user trust

---

# Evaluation Principles

- Repeatable
- Transparent
- Quantitative where possible
- Human-reviewed for critical cases
- Continuous improvement

---

# Evaluation Categories

## Reasoning Accuracy

Measure whether the AI:

- Correctly understands user intent
- Applies trading concepts logically
- Explains conclusions step-by-step
- Avoids unsupported assumptions

---

## Knowledge Retrieval

Evaluate:

- Retrieval relevance
- Citation of internal knowledge
- Context completeness
- Missing information rate

---

## Strategy Rule Adherence

Verify the AI consistently follows documented strategy rules:

- HTF bias
- Market structure
- Liquidity
- BOS / ChoCH
- Entry checklist
- Risk management

---

## Response Quality

Assess:

- Clarity
- Accuracy
- Structure
- Educational value
- Consistency of tone

---

## Performance Metrics

Track:

- First-token latency
- Total response time
- Streaming stability
- Retrieval time
- Error rate

---

# Benchmark Suite

Maintain a fixed library of benchmark prompts covering:

- Beginner questions
- Live trade analysis
- Journal review
- Psychology coaching
- Strategy explanations
- Knowledge lookups

Compare results after every major update.

---

# Human Review

Review samples for:

- Hallucinations
- Unsafe advice
- Strategy violations
- Missing context
- Poor explanations

Record findings and corrective actions.

---

# User Feedback

Collect:

- Thumbs up / down
- Written feedback
- Suggested corrections
- Satisfaction score

Use feedback to prioritize improvements.

---

# Continuous Improvement Cycle

Benchmark
→ Evaluate
→ Analyze
→ Improve
→ Re-test
→ Deploy

---

# Success Criteria

- Higher benchmark scores
- Fewer hallucinations
- Better retrieval accuracy
- Faster responses
- Increased user satisfaction

---

# Long-Term Vision

AI evaluation becomes an automated quality pipeline that continuously validates every release, ensuring Traders World AI remains accurate, explainable, and aligned with its documented trading methodology.
