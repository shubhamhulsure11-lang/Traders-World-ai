# Traders World AI — Backtest & Analytics Engine

## Purpose

The Backtest Engine transforms historical trading data into actionable knowledge.

Its purpose is not just to calculate statistics, but to help the AI learn from every historical trade and improve future coaching.

---

# Objectives

- Measure strategy performance.
- Discover recurring patterns.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Build confidence using historical evidence.
- Feed verified knowledge into the AI Copilot.

---

# Data Sources

The engine should support:

- Manual trade entries
- CSV imports
- Trading journals
- Screenshots
- TradingView exports
- Future broker integrations

---

# Trade Record

Every trade should include:

- Date & Time
- Symbol
- Session
- Direction
- HTF Bias
- LTF Confirmation
- Entry Price
- Stop Loss
- Take Profit
- Risk %
- Result (Win/Loss)
- R-Multiple
- Screenshot
- Notes
- Emotions
- Rules Followed
- Rules Broken

---

# Performance Metrics

Calculate:

- Win Rate
- Loss Rate
- Profit Factor
- Average RR
- Expectancy
- Average Holding Time
- Max Drawdown
- Consecutive Wins/Losses
- Best Session
- Best Setup Type

---

# AI Questions It Should Answer

Examples:

- Show all A+ trades.
- Which setup has the highest win rate?
- What mistakes do I repeat?
- Which session is most profitable?
- How often do I break my rules?
- Show similar historical trades.

---

# Learning Workflow

Trade Completed
        ↓
Journal Created
        ↓
Backtest Database Updated
        ↓
Statistics Recalculated
        ↓
Knowledge Base Updated
        ↓
AI Uses New Knowledge

Every completed trade makes the AI more informed.
