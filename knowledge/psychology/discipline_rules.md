# Trading Psychology & Emotional Discipline Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Max Loss Limit**: Cease trading for the day after 2 consecutive losses or a 2% daily loss limit.
2. **No Revenge Trading**: Taking a trade within 15 minutes of a loss is classified as revenge trading and violates risk management.
3. **No FOMO Entries**: Chasing price outside the predefined entry zone or after displacement has occurred is prohibited.
4. **Execution Protocol**: Execute only setups that meet all 8 rules on the pre-trade checklist.
