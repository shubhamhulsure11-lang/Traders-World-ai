# Rule 04: Lower Timeframe Structure Shift (ChoCH / BOS)

## Statement
Lower Timeframe (LTF) entry requires a explicit Change of Character (ChoCH) or Break of Structure (BOS) on the 1m or 5m timeframe with a full candle body close beyond the recent swing low/high.

## Justification
Wicks indicate liquidity sweeps, whereas candle body closes indicate real structural shift and direction commitment by market participants.

## Execution Rules
- Do not enter on market orders inside an order block without a 1m/5m ChoCH.
- Wait for a return to the LTF Order Block or FVG created by the ChoCH displacement leg.
