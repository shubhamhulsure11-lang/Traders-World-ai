# Rule 03: Liquidity Sweep Before Entry

## Statement
No setup is valid unless liquidity resting above equal highs (BSL) or below equal lows (SSL) has been swept before price arrives at the key zone.

## Justification
Institutional orders require high liquidity pools to fill large position sizes. Entering before a liquidity sweep increases the probability of being stopped out by a stop run.

## Rule Validation Checklist
- Identify BSL (Buy-Side Liquidity) or SSL (Sell-Side Liquidity) on 15m/1H charts.
- Verify price has pierced the liquidity pool with a wick or swift displacement.
- Ensure structure shift follows immediately after the sweep.
