# Higher Timeframe Bias Strategy

## Overview
Higher Timeframe (HTF) bias dictates the macro direction of the market.
Never execute lower timeframe trades against the HTF trend.

## Key Rules
1. Identify trend on Daily and 4-Hour charts (Higher Highs & Higher Lows = Bullish, Lower Highs & Lower Lows = Bearish).
2. Mark key supply and demand zones on 4-Hour and 1-Hour charts.
3. Determine if price is in Premium (sell zone) or Discount (buy zone).
4. Do NOT enter a trade on 1-minute or 5-minute charts until price enters a valid HTF zone.
