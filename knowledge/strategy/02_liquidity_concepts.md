# Smart Money Liquidity Concepts

## Buy-Side & Sell-Side Liquidity
Liquidity exists where retail trader stop losses reside.
- **Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL)**: Stop-loss buy orders above equal highs or previous day highs.
- **Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL)**: Stop-loss sell orders below equal lows or previous day lows.

## Inducement (IDM)
An inducement is a minor structural high/low designed to trap premature retail traders into entering setups before the true key zone is reached.
Never enter on an inducement high/low. Always target entry at the unmitigated Point of Interest (POI) behind the inducement.
