# Glossary: Smart Money Concepts

## Liquidity
Pools of stop-loss orders resting above equal highs or below equal lows. Institutions target these pools to fill massive position orders.

## BOS (Break of Structure)
A candle close beyond a recent swing high or low in the direction of the existing trend, confirming trend continuation.

## ChoCH (Change of Character)
The first structural break against the current micro-trend, signaling a potential trend reversal.

## A+ Zone
A high-probability interest area combining HTF order block, fair value gap (FVG), and liquidity sweep.
